## Differences to review

### 1. Structure
- **PPT only:** Cover + 7 content slides + **A1–A7 visual format references** (community, AMA, paid, UGC, press, interview, offline).
- **DOCX only:** Continuous memo; **no appendix / visual references**.

### 2. Framing / emphasis
- **DOCX:** Core message framed as **“크립토 네이티브 신용 창출”**; funds flow to fintech lenders.
- **PPT:** “3Jane funds fintech credit” + LCC launch; less explicit “crypto-native credit creation” headline.
- **DOCX:** Explicitly **excludes** broad reward campaigns and **borrower acquisition** from early priority.
- **PPT:** Focuses on supplier interest; does **not** spell out those exclusions as clearly.

### 3. Product / LCC detail
- **DOCX LCC:** commitment fee, capital-call mechanics, deadlines, margin loss risk (more detail).
- **PPT LCC:** margin backs larger funding commitment; funding deadlines, liquidity, margin slashing (shorter).
- **DOCX:** Names USD3 / sUSD3 / LCC together in the problem section.
- **PPT:** LCC on “current position”; USD3/sUSD3 mainly on messaging slide.

### 4. Commercial / ops language
- **DOCX:** Taxes, payment fees inside approved envelope or separate written approval; **2 business-day** review window; written approval for major reallocation / total increase; product-change → pause CTAs.
- **PPT:** Kickoff confirms milestone, owners, quotations, payment terms; thinner on change-control and fee handling.
- **PPT:** “Partner scope follows quotations. Event booking follows qualified demand.”
- **DOCX:** Same idea for Seoul event (“유효 수요가 뒷받침되면”), but fee/change-control language is stronger.

### 5. Measurement detail
- **DOCX:** Weekly dashboard + monthly review; bot/duplicate removal; consented leads; reconcile product actions; final report + budget reconciliation within **10 business days**; if product unavailable → keep education, **hold conversion spend**.
- **PPT:** Shorter KPI + reserve controls; does not list 10-business-day closeout or bot/dupe rules on-slide.

### 6. Slide numbering quirk (PPT only)
Slide numbers in the HOPE deck are remapped from a longer deck (e.g. budget slide labeled **07**, roadmap **04**, priority **02**). Content is fine; labels look out of order vs DOCX flow.

### 7. Wording / localization notes (not contradictions)
- DOCX already Korean; brand terms kept (3Jane, USD3, sUSD3, LCC, HOPE, AMA, JANE, APY, X, DeFi).
- PPT KO keeps **Operation** in English on the advisory label (`Operation & 별도 자문`) per prior request.
- DOCX uses **운영** in Korean for program operations (e.g. “3개월 운영 관리”) — different sense from the Operation advisory label.
